# Sources for the lecture 6 gallery of gridded data

Every figure in this folder except `gridded_data_scales.png` is reproduced
unmodified from an article published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Each file is the publisher's largest figure file, downloaded on 2026-10-12 and
not cropped or edited; the license was confirmed on each article page.
`gridded_data_scales.png` is drawn by `figscripts/gridded_data_scales.py` from the
grid spacings and extents stated in the papers below and in the candidate list
of the same search.
